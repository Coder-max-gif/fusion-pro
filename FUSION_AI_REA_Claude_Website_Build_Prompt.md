# FUSION AI — Website Build Brief for REA + Claude

## 1. Project objective

Build a premium, futuristic website for **FUSION AI**, an AI-powered trading platform concept.

The website should communicate a platform with three distinct AI trading bots / plans. Each plan has a different proposed monthly return target and risk profile:

| Plan | Proposed monthly target | Risk level | Risk description |
|---|---:|---|---|
| FUSION SAFE | 1–2% | Low | Lower-risk strategy profile; losses are still possible |
| FUSION CORE | 5–10% | Moderate | Higher volatility and a greater possibility of losses |
| FUSION NOVA | 10–15% | High | Very high risk; the trading account could lose a substantial amount or be completely lost |

**Critical financial-content rule:** These percentages are proposed targets/concept figures from the brief, not verified historical performance, guaranteed returns, promises, or forecasts. Never imply that a user will earn these returns. Label them clearly as illustrative/proposed monthly targets, add prominent risk disclosures, and do not invent audited performance, testimonials, client counts, win rates, regulatory approvals, licenses, or partner logos. Include a clear statement that trading can result in partial or total loss of capital. The business owner must obtain legal/compliance review for the jurisdictions in which the service will operate before publishing or accepting funds.

## 2. Creative direction

Create an original visual identity for FUSION AI. The experience should feel like a premium interactive technology product—not a generic AI landing page, crypto template, casino, or ordinary finance website.

### Visual inspiration
- The playful, character-led energy and game-like presentation of Clash of Clans.
- Cinematic asteroid fields, deep space, planets, orbiting particles, energy trails and atmospheric lighting.
- Premium sci-fi interfaces, sophisticated dashboards and polished product storytelling.
- High-end interactive agency websites with scroll-linked animation and carefully controlled motion.

Use these only as broad inspiration. Do not copy copyrighted characters, assets, logos, layouts, or distinctive artwork from Clash of Clans or any other reference.

### Art direction
- Dark space background: near-black, deep navy and subtle blue-violet gradients.
- Accent palette: electric blue, cyan, violet and restrained warm highlights.
- Strong typography with a distinctive display font and highly readable body font.
- Rich depth, soft bloom, glass-like panels, subtle borders and controlled glow.
- Large, cinematic compositions with generous negative space.
- Each of the three bots must have a distinct silhouette, personality, color accent and animation.
- Avoid excessive neon, random gradients, generic AI illustrations, clutter and overused glassmorphism.

## 3. The three AI bots

Create three original 3D-style bot characters. They should feel like a coherent family of intelligent machines, not three unrelated stock robots.

### Bot 1 — FUSION SAFE
- Proposed monthly target: 1–2%.
- Risk label: LOW.
- Character: calm, protective, precise, stable.
- Visual language: shield, controlled energy field, cool blue/cyan light.
- Short description: designed around a more conservative trading approach; returns are not assured and losses remain possible.

### Bot 2 — FUSION CORE
- Proposed monthly target: 5–10%.
- Risk label: MODERATE.
- Character: analytical, adaptive, balanced and responsive.
- Visual language: rotating energy core, data rings, violet/blue light.
- Short description: a more active strategy profile with increased volatility and risk.

### Bot 3 — FUSION NOVA
- Proposed monthly target: 10–15%.
- Risk label: HIGH.
- Character: powerful, aggressive, high-energy and unpredictable.
- Visual language: radiant star/asteroid energy, orange-violet highlights, controlled particle bursts.
- Short description: an aggressive strategy profile with substantial risk; users could lose all capital allocated to the trading account.

Do not portray any bot as guaranteeing profits or autonomously producing a verified return.

## 4. Website structure and content

Build a complete multi-section landing page with the following sequence.

### A. Navigation
- FUSION AI wordmark / logo.
- Links: Home, Technology, AI Bots, Risk, FAQ.
- Primary CTA: “Explore Plans”.
- Responsive mobile navigation.
- Sticky navigation that changes subtly after scrolling.

### B. Hero — “Intelligence in Motion”
Create a cinematic first impression.

Suggested copy:
- Eyebrow: “THE NEXT EVOLUTION OF ALGORITHMIC TRADING”
- Main heading: “Three Minds. One Fusion.”
- Supporting text: “Explore a new generation of AI-assisted trading strategies, designed around distinct approaches to risk and market participation.”
- CTA 1: “Explore the Bots”
- CTA 2: “Understand the Risk”

Visual:
- A large central futuristic AI core or original bot.
- Asteroids and particles moving slowly in depth.
- Subtle orbital rings and data signals.
- A cinematic reveal as the user enters the page.
- Ensure text remains legible and the hero remains performant on mobile.

### C. Introduction / platform story
Explain what FUSION AI is without inventing technical facts.

Suggested copy:
“FUSION AI brings three distinct trading strategy profiles into one experience. Each bot represents a different balance of target, activity and risk. Explore the approach behind each profile before deciding whether it is appropriate for you.”

Use animated diagrams or visual storytelling to explain the three profiles. Do not claim machine-learning capabilities, proprietary algorithms, broker integrations, live execution, or audited results unless the owner later supplies evidence.

### D. The three bots / plans
This is the most important section.

Display three visually distinctive plan cards or large character-led panels. Each must include:
- Bot name.
- Proposed monthly target, clearly labelled “Illustrative target — not guaranteed”.
- Risk level.
- Concise strategy-profile description.
- A clear risk statement.
- CTA: “Explore this bot” or “View details”.

Make the plans interactive:
- Hover: bot reacts, glows or animates.
- Click: open a detail panel/modal or scroll to its expanded information.
- Scroll: each bot enters with a distinct but coordinated animation.
- On mobile: cards stack cleanly and remain easy to use.

Do not add pricing, minimum deposits, subscription costs, account sizes, leverage, lock-in periods, or payment buttons. Those details were not provided.

### E. Compare the profiles
Create a clear comparison area with:
- Bot name.
- Proposed target range.
- Risk level.
- General strategy profile.
- Potential downside.

Keep the risk differences clear. Do not invent exact drawdown percentages, risk scores, trade frequency, win rates or stop-loss values.

### F. How it works
Use a simple visual sequence, but only describe a conceptual journey:
1. Explore the three strategy profiles.
2. Understand the risk and limitations.
3. Review the available product information.
4. Make an informed decision.

Do not imply that account creation, deposits, automated execution, broker connection or withdrawals are already available unless they are actually implemented.

### G. Technology / intelligence section
Create a visually engaging section that communicates the concept of algorithmic trading and data-driven analysis.

Possible visual elements:
- Abstract market data streams.
- Animated chart-like lines.
- Network nodes.
- Orbital data structures.
- AI core visualization.

Keep all claims conceptual until technical documentation is supplied. Do not fabricate architecture, data sources, backtest results, live market feeds, security certifications or AI model capabilities.

### H. Risk disclosure section
This section must be prominent, understandable and accessible—not hidden in the footer.

Suggested copy:
“Trading involves substantial risk. The proposed monthly target ranges shown on this website are illustrative figures supplied for describing the three strategy profiles. They are not guaranteed returns, promises, or representations of actual performance. Actual results may be negative. You may lose some or all of the capital allocated to trading. The high-risk FUSION NOVA profile carries a particularly elevated risk of substantial or total account loss. Past performance, if supplied in the future, would not guarantee future results. Only consider trading with money you can afford to lose, and seek independent financial advice where appropriate.”

Include a clear acknowledgement that the content is informational and is not individualized investment advice. Have the final wording reviewed by qualified legal/compliance professionals before launch.

### I. FAQ
Include functional accordion items:
- Are the monthly targets guaranteed?
- Can I lose money?
- What is the difference between the three bots?
- Which plan has the highest risk?
- Can the high-risk account be completely lost?
- Is this financial advice?
- Where can I find verified performance information?

Answers must follow the risk rules above. Do not invent missing product details.

### J. Footer
- FUSION AI brand.
- Navigation links.
- Risk disclosure link.
- Privacy Policy and Terms links as clearly marked placeholders until approved legal copy is supplied.
- Copyright line with a neutral placeholder if the company’s legal entity and year are not confirmed.
- Contact details must be placeholders, not fabricated.

## 5. Interaction and motion requirements

The website must feel alive but remain usable.

Implement:
- Smooth, intentional scrolling.
- Scroll-triggered reveals.
- Bot character idle animations.
- Subtle parallax on asteroids and background particles.
- Hover and click feedback on cards and buttons.
- Animated number/range reveals, without suggesting live or guaranteed returns.
- Functional FAQ accordions.
- Functional mobile menu.
- Working section navigation and CTA anchors.
- Accessible focus states and reduced-motion support.

Motion must be purposeful. Avoid constant movement behind paragraphs, excessive scroll hijacking, long intro loaders, cursor gimmicks that obstruct usability, or animations that make mobile performance poor.

## 6. Functional frontend requirements

The result must be a complete, runnable frontend—not a static mockup.

Required:
- All navigation links work.
- All CTA buttons have meaningful destinations or actions.
- Bot cards open the correct detail content.
- FAQ accordions open and close.
- Mobile menu works and closes after navigation.
- Responsive layouts work across desktop, tablet and mobile.
- No broken images, missing assets, dead buttons or placeholder links presented as finished features.
- Proper loading, error and empty states wherever relevant.
- Semantic HTML, accessible labels, keyboard navigation and sufficient contrast.
- Reduced-motion preference support.
- Optimized images, lazy loading where appropriate and efficient animations.
- No fake login, checkout, payment, deposit, account dashboard or backend functionality. If such functionality is not supplied, do not simulate it as real.

## 7. Suggested implementation stack

Use the existing project stack if one is already established. Do not replace a working project structure without a reason.

If starting from an empty project, use:
- Next.js with App Router.
- TypeScript.
- Tailwind CSS.
- Framer Motion for UI and scroll animation.
- React components with clean separation.
- CSS 3D / Three.js or React Three Fiber only if genuinely needed for the hero or bot visuals and performance can be maintained.

Keep dependencies lean. Do not add a heavy 3D library merely for decoration. If no genuine 3D model assets are available, create polished original CSS/SVG/canvas-based bot illustrations or use generated assets supplied to the project. Do not use random external image URLs as production assets.

## 8. Design quality checklist

Before considering the frontend complete, verify:
- The site does not look like a generic AI-generated template.
- The first viewport is memorable and clearly communicates FUSION AI.
- The three bots are visually distinct and consistently art-directed.
- The three plan targets and risk levels are easy to compare.
- Risk disclosure is visible and unambiguous.
- The page has a clear visual hierarchy and a coherent story.
- Animation timing feels premium, not distracting.
- All sections work on small screens.
- No unsupported claims or fabricated proof appear anywhere.
- Every visible interactive element works.
